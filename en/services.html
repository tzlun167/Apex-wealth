Our Services

Three Core Mandates

Every mandate begins with the same question: what does this family wish still to remain, a hundred years from now? Our method is not to hand over a list of options, but to arrange those options into an ordered path.

01

Wealth Preservation & AllocationCross-Generational Stewardship

Beginning with the family’s consolidated balance sheet, we integrate holdings scattered across accounts, companies, trusts and jurisdictions into a single picture the family can actually read. On that basis we map succession pathways, address tax and compliance interfaces, and design a liquidity structure able to withstand market cycles and unforeseen events.

Cross-generational succession architecture Tax & compliance planning Structural reorganisation and consolidation Liquidity and risk-boundary setting
02

Family Governance & SuccessionInstitution Before Inheritance

Wealth is the fruit; governance is the cause. We help families institutionalise their decision-making — a family charter, rules of deliberation, and routes for resolving disagreement — and design a development path for successors, so that the next generation holds both the capability and the consensus to carry what it inherits.

Family office establishment Family charter and deliberation rules Next-generation development and handover Facilitation of family meetings
03

Legacy & PhilanthropySpecial Assets & Purpose

Bespoke arrangements for non-standard assets and family purpose. From the institutional design and governance of charitable foundations to the stewardship of art, unlisted equity and family real estate — assets that resist market pricing — we protect both their value and their meaning over the long term.

Charitable foundation design and governance Special asset stewardship Family reputation and external relations Philanthropic succession planning

How We Work

How we work with a family

ⅰ

Initial Conversation

At no cost and with no record created. First, to establish whether we are suited to one another.

ⅱ

Current-State Review

A survey of assets, family structure and existing arrangements, identifying exposures not yet addressed.

ⅲ

Architecture Design

A sequenced roadmap in which every step carries its stated cost, timing and reversibility.

ⅳ

Long-Term Execution

Reviewed annually and adjusted continuously as family membership and regulation evolve.

We do not provide lists of options; we provide sequence. Most families do not suffer from a shortage of information, but from not knowing which step comes first.

If you would like to know whether these services suit your family, we suggest beginning with a fully confidential initial conversation.
