Security & Privacy

Privacy is the
premise of this service.

A family’s wealth structure, health, internal disputes and succession arrangements are the most sensitive class of information there is. We treat keeping it from leaving as a core competency to be built — not a clause appended to a contract.

Our Safeguards

Six Lines of Defence

Least Privilege

Any colleague may access only the data strictly needed for their work. Cross-family access is prohibited without exception or special approval.

Full Audit Trail

Every read, modification and export of data leaves a tamper-resistant record. A family may request an audit report within an agreed cycle.

Information Isolation

Different families’ data are separated both logically and physically. Environments operate independently, with no shared storage or backup paths.

Confidentiality Undertakings

Confidentiality obligations bind all advisers, external professional firms and contractors, and extend well beyond the end of an engagement.

Communications Protection

Transmission is protected by strong encryption throughout; confidential documents are encrypted in storage, with keys held separately.

Strict Confidentiality

We do not discuss clients, appear in endorsements, or place a family’s name in any promotional material or case study.

Data Principles

How we handle your information

Every item you provide is used only for the service purposes described on this site. We do not use it for marketing, do not provide it to third parties for their own use, and do not transfer it across borders beyond what the service strictly requires, absent your written consent.

Purpose Limitation

Used solely for consultation and service provision — never for product recommendation or marketing.

Retention Period

Where no engagement results, information is deleted after the agreed period following the conversation.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data you have provided, at any time.

Disclosure

Necessary disclosures

This website exists solely to describe our services and enable contact by appointment. Nothing on it constitutes investment advice, an offer or a solicitation in respect of any financial instrument, nor does it guarantee any future return.

Any service relationship arises only upon execution of a written engagement contract by both parties. Before engagement we disclose in full the scope of services, the fee structure and any potential conflicts of interest.
